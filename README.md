Entry for the [make.fun bounty "One Line, One Chill"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-one-line-one-chill-6d4d5488) — a single sentence of horror, under 200 characters, that has to frighten a reader in one breath.

## The line

> I buried my dog on Tuesday, and every night since, something has scratched at the back door in the exact rhythm of his tail wagging — and tonight it knocked.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`) and prints it to stdout. No network, no dependencies, deterministic.
